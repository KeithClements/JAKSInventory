How the 10 → 6 reorganization works

  • CORE PROCESSING dissolves: Customer Cores moves into INVENTORY (it's stock you owe to customers). Vendor Returns moves into PURCHASING (it's the inverse of receiving).
  • PRICING merges into INVENTORY: price is a product attribute. Three pricing sub-tabs become sub-tabs of Inventory.
  • MARKETING merges into CUSTOMERS: messaging, campaigns, and automation all target customers. Same area, same audience.
  • TOOLS demotes to the gear menu: these are admin utilities, not daily-driver workflows. One click from anywhere.
  • SETTINGS stays in the gear menu with a refreshed dark layout (Wave 8 in the tracker).

Dashboard redesign goals

  • Greet + Quick Actions strip — the first thing you see is what you can do, not what's broken.
  • Critical Alerts only when critical — collapse zero-value alert cards into a single "All clear" pill. No more wall of red zeros.
  • Pipeline grid stays — it's the heart of the screen — but tighter, fewer cards, more meaningful numbers (revenue today, gross margin today).
  • Activity feed — replaces empty space. Last 20 events across the app (sale recorded, PO received, payment posted, QBO sync, etc.).
  • Analytics row stays — Revenue, Quote Conversion, Top Customers. Same charts, better framing.
  • Status bar — QBO pill + sync queue + last refresh, always visible.
