{# macros/line_thumb.html ====================== Small product image thumbnail for line-item rows on the quote / sales-order / invoice workspaces. Mirrors the preview-dock thumbnail (products/_preview_panel.html) but smaller and with object-contain so wide parts (injectors, manifolds, etc.) are shown whole rather than cropped. Renders nothing when the line has no product or the product has no image — child lines (core, warranty, upgrade, optional, misc) stay clean.
