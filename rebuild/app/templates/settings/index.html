Reviews & Social Proof

The storefront has almost no reviews while competitors run 800–1,200. The fastest honest fix is to ask the customers who already bought from you. These two lists are built from your finalized sales — one for product reviews (Judge.me), one for company reviews (Google). Customers marked do-not-contact or opted-out are always excluded.

{# Track A — Google / company reviews #}

1 · Google reviews (the trust signal buyers check first)

A personal-ask list of your best customers (highest spend first) with per-channel consent flags. Set up a Google Business Profile, grab your review link, then email/text these folks. Google forbids incentives for its reviews — just ask.

Download Google outreach list (CSV)
{# Track B — Judge.me / product reviews #}

2 · Product reviews (puts star ratings on product pages)

Ready to upload to Judge.me (Shopify): Settings → Review requests → import CSV. One row per customer + product they bought, deduped to the most recent purchase. Only includes products already linked to Shopify and customers who allow email. Your product pages already render the star schema — they just need data.

Download Judge.me request CSV

Ready-to-use email & text copy lives in app/messaging_templates/review_request_email.txt and review_request_sms.txt. Keep asking every customer going forward — a weekly export turns past sales into a steady stream of reviews.
