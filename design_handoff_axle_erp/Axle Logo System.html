The Axle Mark

Direction 01 — the keyed hub — finalized into a working logo system. Concentric races read precision; the amber bore is the center every part turns on. Everything here is built to survive a 256px sidebar, a 16px favicon, and a black-and-white printed invoice.

01

The Mark

anatomy · construction
  • Outer race — heaviest stroke. The structural ring; the only element that must read at favicon size.
  • Inner race — lighter stroke. Adds the bearing/tolerance read; drops out below 24px.
  • Bore — amber. The single accent — the center everything aligns to. Never more than one accent in the mark.
  • Keyway — the notch at top. The detail that makes it a keyed shaft, not just rings. Optional below 32px.
